No house style stamped onto every head. Just deliberate work, tailored to you and built to last between visits.

Our story

Inside the atelier

A small studio in the city, built on the idea that great hair is craft — not a conveyor belt.

Since 2014

Founded on a
quieter kind of luxury.

MÈCHE began when our founder, frustrated by rushed appointments and one-size styling, opened a studio with only a handful of chairs. The rule was simple: never book more clients than you can give your full attention.

A decade on, that hasn't changed. The lighting is warm, the coffee is good, the playlist is considered — and the work speaks for itself. We'd rather be a little harder to get into than rush the thing you'll wear every day.

What we hold to

Three house rules

✦

Time, given fully

Every appointment gets its full window. No double-booking, no rushing you out the door for the next head.

✦

Honesty over upsell

If a look won't suit your hair or your routine, we'll tell you — and suggest what will. Trust outlasts a single sale.

✦

Care that lasts

We build cuts and colour that grow out gracefully, so you look intentional between visits, not just on the day.
