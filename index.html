Cosmos

A personal tool for publishing videos to TikTok.

Cosmos lets a creator connect their own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit, choose a video from their device, set a title and privacy level, and publish it to their own TikTok profile using the TikTok Content Posting API. The creator reviews and confirms every post before it is sent.
