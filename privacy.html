Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1Overview

Cosmos ("the App") publishes videos to a creator's own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

2Information we handle

  • TikTok account information: when you log in with TikTok, we receive an access token and refresh token, and basic profile information (such as your display name and avatar) permitted by the scopes you approve.
  • Videos and post details: the video file, title, and privacy settings you choose to publish.

We do not collect your TikTok password. Login happens on TikTok's own page.

3How we use it

We use this information only to authenticate you with TikTok and to publish the content you select to your TikTok account. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Your videos and post details are sent only to TikTok, at your request, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

5Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as you keep the App connected. Video files are used only to complete the upload and are not kept afterwards. You can request deletion of any stored data at any time.

6Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions. Disconnecting revokes our access. To request deletion of data we hold, contact us below.

7Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

9Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: dudecosmo@gmail.com
